Isoperia is in active development and playable now. This is the honest version
of where it is going: what already works, what is being built, and what is still
an intention rather than a plan. There are no dates on it. Everything below can
move, and the order is more meaningful than the timing.

## Playable today

**A world you walk through.** A third-person camera you can orbit and zoom,
proper collision and gravity, and direct interaction with whatever you are
looking at. The mainland renders as one continuous landscape rather than a grid
of tiles.

**The settlement loop.** Gather raw materials, build a sawmill and a smelter,
craft planks, bars, tools and armour, and push further out. Assign villagers to
tasks and the settlement keeps producing while you are away.

**Combat with decisions in it.** An attack style chosen per fight, a Resolve
pool spent on precision, power or warding, a charged special on every weapon,
and monster affixes that change how a fight goes. Losing costs you part of what
you were carrying.

**Somewhere to go.** Hearthvale as a settlement with real buildings and roads,
connected biome landmarks to steer by, attunable return waystones, and the
Cinder Hollow expedition out in the deep east.

**Saves that survive.** Progress is reconciled authoritatively, so a closed tab
or a lost connection returns you to a state the game agrees with. Saves can be
exported and imported.

## In progress

**Making the world readable at a glance.** Consistent lighting and materials, a
clear silhouette for every landmark, and terrain that tells you what kind of
place you are standing in before you read a single label.

**Character motion worth watching.** Idle, walk, run, gather, attack, hurt and
defeat, driven by what is actually happening rather than approximated.

**Cinder Hollow as a real journey.** The expedition exists; the approach to it,
the pacing inside it and the way out are being built into something you would
describe to someone else afterwards.

**Sound, and running well on a phone.** Ambient beds, interaction and combat
audio, and the device work that makes a browser game on a mid-range phone
pleasant rather than merely possible.

## Planned

**Connected biomes.** The regions joined into one continuous world you can cross
on foot, with the reason to cross it built into what is on the other side.

**A second expedition and a boss ladder.** Somewhere to go once Cinder Hollow
stops being frightening, and something at the end of it worth preparing for.

**Economy and endgame.** Trade, higher-tier crafting, and goals that outlast the
first settlement.

**A wider test release.** Onboarding, save recovery and enough polish that
handing the link to someone who has never seen it is a fair thing to do.

## What is not planned

Being straight about this is more useful than leaving it open.

**No accounts, and no cloud saves yet.** Your progress lives in your browser.
That is why export and import exist, and it is the honest reason to use them.

**Nothing to buy.** There are no purchases, no currency and no advertising in
the game, and none are planned.

**No multiplayer.** Isoperia is a single-player game. Anything that changed that
would be a different project rather than a later phase of this one.
